### User Interaction & Social Features

**Q: Does the app allow users to interact, communicate, or exchange content with other users?**
**Answer:** Yes — limited.

**Details:** Customers can send text messages to the restaurant they are ordering from (e.g., "no onions please," delivery instructions, order clarifications). This is a one-to-one channel between the customer and the merchant fulfilling their order. There is no user-to-user chat, no public forums, no social feed, no friend lists, and no broadcast messaging.

**Q: Does the app share the user's location with other users?**
**Answer:** No. Location is shared only with the merchant fulfilling the order and with the assigned delivery driver, strictly for delivery fulfillment — not with other consumers.

**Q: Can users upload or share user-generated content?**
**Answer:** Yes — minimal. Users may submit order notes (free-text instructions to the restaurant) and optionally a profile photo (visible only to the merchant/driver fulfilling their order). Optional ratings/reviews of completed orders may be submitted as text.

**Q: Is user-generated content moderated?**
**Answer:** Yes — order notes and reviews are reviewed by the restaurant operator before being acted on or displayed. Reported content is reviewed by Barmagly support. Users can be blocked for abuse.

## Section 2 — Target Audience and Content

### Target Age Group

**Q: What is the target age group for your app?**
**Answer:** Select **only the following age band(s):**

- 13–15 — **No**
- 16–17 — **No**
- 18 and over — **YES** (primary)

Tick **only "18 and over"** as the primary target audience. Do NOT tick any age bracket under 13.

**Rationale:** The app facilitates real-money purchases of prepared food and delivery service. Account creation, payment, and delivery address management are operations appropriate for adults. The minimum stated user age is 13 (Terms of Service), but the **intended and marketed target audience is adults 18+** who can independently order food, manage payment, and accept deliveries.

### Appeal to Children

**Q: Is your app designed for children or does it unintentionally appeal to children?**
**Answer:** **No — the app is not designed for children and does not appeal to children.**

**Q: Does your app's store listing (icon, screenshots, description, title) contain imagery, characters, themes, or language that would be especially appealing to children?**
**Answer:** No. The store listing uses a dark theme (#070A12), restaurant photography, an orange (#FF5722) brand accent, and adult-oriented UI copy. There are no cartoon mascots, no toys, no child-targeted characters, no bright primary colors used in a child-app pattern, no kid-friendly fonts, and no gamification elements aimed at children.

**Q: Does your app contain any content that may be especially appealing to children, even if not designed for them?**
**Answer:** No.

---

### Children Mistakenly Downloading the App

**Q: Could children mistakenly download your app?**
**Answer:** Unlikely. The app is listed in the **Food & Drink** category, requires account creation with a phone number, requires a delivery address, and supports real-money purchases — all of which require adult capability. Branding, store listing, and UX are oriented toward adult consumers.

**Q: What measures have you taken to prevent children from using the app?**
**Answer:**
- Minimum age 13 enforced in the Terms of Service (acceptance required at registration).
- Account creation requires a valid phone number (SMS verification) and/or Google Sign-In, both of which presume an adult-managed account.
- Real-money transactions require a payment method, which children typically cannot independently provide.
- Delivery requires a real physical address and (in many cases) in-person handoff to an adult.
- App branding, copy, and visual design are deliberately adult-oriented (dark UI, no cartoon imagery).
- The app contains no features that are targeted at, gamified for, or marketed to children.
