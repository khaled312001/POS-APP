## Long-form changelog

### Barmagly Customer 1.0.0 — Initial release

This is the first public release of Barmagly Customer, the diner-facing companion to the Barmagly point-of-sale platform used by restaurants across Switzerland, Germany, Egypt and Saudi Arabia.

#### What's included

**Ordering**
- Browse the full menu of any restaurant on the Barmagly platform
- Add items to your cart with modifiers, options and special instructions
- Choose between pickup and delivery at checkout
- Pay through the methods supported by each venue
- Order history with one-tap reorder for your favourites

**Delivery**
- Real-time order tracking from kitchen to your door
- Live driver location on the map once your order is en route
- Status updates at every step: received, preparing, ready, out for delivery, delivered
- Save multiple delivery addresses (home, work, etc.) for faster checkout
- Optional precise location to help drivers find you

**Account**
- Sign in with Google for one-tap access
- Manage your profile, phone number and delivery addresses
- Optional profile photo (camera or photo library)
- Order history synced across devices

**Communication**
- In-app chat with the restaurant for order questions, changes or special requests
- Push-notification ready for order status updates (where supported)

**Languages**
- English, German, Arabic (with right-to-left layout) and French
- Automatic language detection from your device settings, switchable in-app

**Design**
- Dark UI tuned for evening browsing, with the Barmagly orange accent
- Optimised for phones and tablets in portrait and landscape

#### Compatibility

- Android 8.0 Oreo (API level 26) and above
- Requires Google Play Services for Google Sign-In and maps
- Works on phones and tablets
- Approximately 40 MB install size

#### Known limitations

- **Internet required** — Barmagly Customer is an online app. Menus, orders, tracking and chat all need a live connection to your restaurant's Barmagly back-office. There is no offline mode in version 1.0; cached menus and an offline order draft are on the roadmap for a future release.
- Push notifications depend on the restaurant having notifications enabled on their POS configuration.
- Delivery tracking accuracy depends on the driver's device GPS signal.

#### About Barmagly

Barmagly (www.barmagly.tech) builds the point-of-sale platform that powers your favourite local restaurants. Every order you place in this app lands directly in the kitchen's POS — no third-party marketplace, no inflated fees, no middleman taking a cut from the restaurant. The restaurant pays the SaaS subscription so the app stays free for you.

Questions, feedback or trouble with an order? Reach us at barmaglyy@gmail.com or chat directly with the restaurant from inside the app.
