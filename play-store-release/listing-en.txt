=== APP NAME ===
Barmagly POS

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION ===
Tablet POS & mini-ERP for pharmacies, retail, cafes & restaurants. CHF-ready.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
Running a busy counter shouldn't mean juggling three apps, a spreadsheet, and a notebook. Barmagly POS replaces them all with one tablet-first point of sale and mini-ERP built for pharmacies, retail shops, cafes, restaurants, and growing small businesses across Switzerland and Europe.

Sell faster, track every item, understand your numbers, and accept the payment methods your customers actually use — including TWINT, card, and cash — from a single screen.

POINT OF SALE
- Lightning-fast tablet checkout with a clean, dark-mode interface
- Item modifiers, sizes, add-ons, and order notes
- Line-item and order-level discounts
- Camera barcode scanning — no extra hardware required
- Park, recall, split, and merge orders
- Table management for restaurants and cafes

INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
- Unlimited products, categories, and variants
- Real-time stock levels across branches
- Low-stock alerts and reorder thresholds
- Expiry tracking for pharmacy and grocery
- Bulk import and image uploads
- Cost, margin, and supplier tracking

CUSTOMERS & CRM
- Customer profiles with full order history
- Loyalty-ready data: phone, email, addresses
- Delivery contacts saved for one-tap reorders
- Notes and tags for VIPs and regulars

REPORTS & ANALYTICS
- Daily, weekly, and custom-range sales reports
- Inventory, returns, finance, and staff activity reports
- Top products, top categories, and peak hours
- Cashier shift and Z-reports
- Export-ready data for your accountant

ONLINE ORDERS & DELIVERY
- QR-code table ordering for cafes and restaurants
- Online menu with live availability
- Delivery zones with per-area pricing
- Promo codes and scheduled offers
- Built-in driver management and dispatch
- WhatsApp and email order notifications

MULTI-BRANCH & TEAM
- Run multiple locations from one account
- Role-based access for Admin, Manager, and Cashier
- PIN login for staff — fast and secure
- Centralized menu and pricing with per-branch overrides
- Activity log for full accountability

PAYMENTS & RECEIPTS
- Cash, card, TWINT, and Stripe
- Multi-currency with Swiss Franc (CHF) as default
- Split payments and partial refunds
- Bluetooth and network receipt printing
- Digital receipts via email or WhatsApp

SECURITY & RELIABILITY
- Each store is fully isolated — multi-tenant by design
- License-key activation (BARMAGLY-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX)
- Encrypted HTTPS connection to pos.barmagly.tech
- No third-party advertising, no analytics SDKs by default
- Hosted on Barmagly's own European infrastructure

WHO IT'S FOR
- Pharmacies needing batch and expiry control
- Retail stores managing stock across shelves and branches
- Cafes and restaurants running tables, takeaway, and delivery
- Independent businesses ready to go digital without enterprise complexity

LANGUAGES
English, German, and Arabic — switch instantly from settings.

PRICING
Free to download. Activate your store with a license key from barmagly.tech. Transparent plans, no hidden fees, no per-transaction surcharges from us.

REQUIREMENTS
- Android tablet or phone (Android 8.0+)
- Internet connection
- Optional: receipt printer, barcode scanner, cash drawer
