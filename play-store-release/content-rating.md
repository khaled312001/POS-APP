**A:** **YES** — for business purpose only (optional, opt-in)

**Q: Does the app collect personal or sensitive user data?**
**A:** **YES** — limited business data only

> **Clarifying note:** The app collects employee login credentials (PIN, email for administrators) for authentication, and customer information (name, phone, delivery address) **entered by the merchant** to process orders. No biometric data, no health data, no advertising identifiers, and no analytics SDKs are used. All data is transmitted over HTTPS to the merchant's tenant on pos.barmagly.tech.

## Summary Declaration

Barmagly POS is a professional Point of Sale and Mini-ERP business application intended exclusively for adult business users (merchants, managers, and employees aged 18 and over) operating retail, hospitality, and pharmacy establishments. The app contains no violence, sexual content, profanity, controlled substance depictions, gambling, advertising, user-generated content, social features, or in-app digital purchases. It collects only the business-operational data necessary to process retail transactions and manage inventory, and all data is transmitted securely to the merchant's own isolated tenant on Barmagly's infrastructure.

**Expected final rating: PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / IARC 3+**
